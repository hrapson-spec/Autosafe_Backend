Check the variant and the individual history

Confirm the specification of each car with its seller. Do not apply the whole-model average as if it were a figure for that exact variant. Read the age-band sample counts and compare the cars' own records for repeat advisories and the sequence of failures, repairs and later tests.

Check the Yaris and Jazz registrations and read the result labels. A recorded pass describes the test on that date; it is not a current condition guarantee. Ask about repair evidence and arrange an inspection for unresolved concerns. The pre-MOT checklist can help with visible checks if you already own one of these cars.
