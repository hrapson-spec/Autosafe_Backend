Before your Corsa's next test

  1. Read the latest MOT record and any earlier repeat advisories. Check repair invoices or ask your garage whether the work was completed.
  2. Find the closest age band below and read its test count alongside the rate. These records combine vehicles with different mileages and histories; they do not isolate your Corsa's condition.
  3. Use the pre-MOT checklist for visible checks. Have warning lights, braking concerns or other faults assessed professionally.

Check a Corsa registration to see available MOT records and a clearly labelled result. Depending on evidence, that result may be an estimate, comparison, reference or unavailable state. None replaces an inspection.
