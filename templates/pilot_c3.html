Read the C3's own record before a viewing

  1. Check the C3 registration and read the dates, recorded mileage, failures and advisories that are available. Ask the seller about gaps or repeated entries rather than assuming their cause.
  2. Ask for evidence of repairs to earlier defects. A later pass is a test result on that date, not a full service history or a guarantee of today's condition.
  3. Use the closest age band below as context. This page does not separate generations, engines or trims, and its component categories are historical records rather than a list of faults on the car for sale.

Arrange an inspection where condition is uncertain. For visible preparation checks, use the car MOT checklist. AutoSafe labels estimates, comparisons, references and unavailable results separately so you can read what the evidence supports.
