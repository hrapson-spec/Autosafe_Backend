Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data.

Garage matching

When you request repair quotes via our garage matching form, we share your contact details (email, name, phone if provided), postcode, vehicle details, and repair description with local garages in your area so they can contact you with quotes. We only share this data when you explicitly consent via the form checkbox.

You can withdraw consent for future handling by emailing autosafehq@gmail.com. Withdrawal cannot recall details already sent; contact a garage directly about its subsequent handling.

Automated decision-making

Our service automatically matches recorded vehicle details, age and available mileage to the closest supported group in aggregated MOT data. It reports that group's recorded failure rate; it does not diagnose this vehicle or determine its next MOT result.

Important:

  • The comparison is for information only
  • It does not guarantee any particular MOT outcome
  • It has no legal effect on you
  • Many factors affect MOT results that we cannot assess
  • You are free to ignore the comparison entirely

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection rights may include the following. These rights are not absolute and legal conditions or exceptions may apply:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability (receive your data in a portable format)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent for garage matching at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at autosafehq@gmail.com.

We will normally respond within one calendar month and will tell you if the law permits more time.

Objecting to legitimate-interest processing: if you object, we will erase your check records or remove the plaintext identifiers and report payload so the retained row cannot directly identify the vehicle without the separately controlled HMAC key, unless we have a compelling legal ground to retain them. Objection does not affect a report already delivered to you. Include the vehicle registration you checked so we can locate your records.

Cookies

We use cookieless Umami Analytics for limited website event statistics. Event payloads use a fixed allowlist and never include registration, postcode, email address or saved-report token. Automatic analytics are disabled on saved-report link routes. Its hosting infrastructure may process network metadata such as an IP address for delivery and security.

Google Ads conversion-measurement cookies are set only if you accept them via the consent banner on your first visit; if you decline, no advertising cookies are set and the site works fully. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser, which will show the banner again.
