MOT Cost: Test Fees, Retests and Repair Quotes

Checked against GOV.UK on 2 October 2026. This guide covers cars in Great Britain.

The maximum MOT test fee for a standard car with up to eight passenger seats is £54.85. A centre may charge less. There is no VAT on the MOT test fee. That price covers the test, not any repairs or a service. See the official MOT fee table for other vehicle classes.

Is an MOT retest free?

The answer depends on where the vehicle stays, when it returns and what failed. GOV.UK sets out the retest conditions:

  • Left at the test centre: a partial retest within 10 working days is free.
  • Taken away and returned by the end of the next working day: a partial retest is free only for the specific items listed by GOV.UK.
  • Taken away and returned within 10 working days: a partial retest may carry a fee.
  • Other circumstances: a full test and full fee apply.

Confirm the arrangement with the original centre before taking the car away. A failed vehicle must still meet the applicable driving and roadworthiness rules; read GOV.UK's guidance after a failed test.

How much will repairs cost?

A model name or historical failure rate cannot provide a repair quote. The fault, parts, labour and work already done all matter. This guide does not estimate an average bill for your car.

Ask the garage for a written breakdown before authorising work:

  1. The test fee and any retest charge, shown separately.
  2. Which failed items need repair and which recommendations concern advisories or optional work.
  3. The parts, labour and VAT included in the repair total, with any diagnostic charge.
  4. Whether the quote is fixed, what could change it, and how additional work will be agreed.

If comparing garages, compare the same work and parts specification. A lower advertised test fee alone does not establish a lower total bill. Earlier MOT advisories can help you ask questions, but they do not prove that a fault remains or give a current repair price.

Questions before your MOT

What is the maximum car MOT test fee?

For a standard car with up to eight passenger seats, the maximum is £54.85. The MOT test fee has no VAT and does not include repairs or servicing.

Is every retest within 10 working days free?

No. A partial retest is free when the vehicle stays at the original test centre and is retested within 10 working days. Taking it away changes the conditions; check the GOV.UK rules above.

Can AutoSafe quote the repairs my car needs?

No. The check tool can help you read available records and labelled evidence. A garage needs to assess the current fault and quote the work.
